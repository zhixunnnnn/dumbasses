# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ESG analysts, investors, and internal reviewers assessing ASEAN-listed utilities. They need to move quickly from a headline score to its evidence, assumptions, model behavior, and source trail.

## Product Purpose

PolyFintech is an ESG evidence and agent workspace. It helps users compare company signals, inspect how ratings and forecasts were assembled, investigate live evidence, and correct agent outputs through a governed review loop. Success means a user can make a defensible decision and trace every material claim to its origin.

## Brand Personality

Analytical, candid, and disciplined. The interface should feel confident without overstating certainty, technical without becoming cryptic, and dense without becoming difficult to scan.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative fintech dashboards, unexplained scores, hidden provenance, overstated model accuracy, ambiguous negative verdicts, and interface copy that presents inferred or illustrative values as measured facts.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before assertion. Every important value should expose its provenance or confidence.
2. Make uncertainty usable. Missing inputs and estimates should remain informative without pretending to be observations.
3. Support fast comparison. Dense analyst workflows need predictable hierarchy, compact controls, and consistent visual encoding.
4. Keep humans in control. Reviews, corrections, and overrides should be visible, reversible, and easy to audit.
5. Prefer honest continuity. When data is incomplete, preserve the workflow with clearly scoped model or scenario views.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic labels, non-color status cues, readable compact type, and reduced-motion preferences.
